Schedule

Two packed days. Plan your time and don't miss your favourite events.

  • 09:00 AM

    Registration and Breakfast

    Collect your ID badge and welcome kit at the help desk.

  • 10:00 AM

    Opening Ceremony and Hackathon Kick-off

    Problem statements are revealed and the 24-hour clock starts.

  • 11:00 AM

    Git and GitHub Workshop

    Hands-on session on branches and pull requests.

  • 01:00 PM

    Lunch Break

    Food court near the auditorium.

  • 02:00 PM

    Code Sprint

    Individual coding contest in Lab 1.

  • 04:00 PM

    Tech Quiz

    Teams of two in Seminar Hall.

  • 08:00 PM

    Hackathon Mentor Round

    Mentors visit every team to give feedback.

  • 09:30 AM

    Intro to AI Workshop

    Build a tiny project with an AI tool.

  • 10:00 AM

    Hackathon Ends • Debug Hunt Starts

    Final hackathon submissions close; Debug Hunt begins in Lab 2.

  • 11:30 AM

    Web Design Battle

    Design a landing page on a surprise theme.

  • 01:00 PM

    Lunch Break

    Food court near the auditorium.

  • 02:00 PM

    Pitch Perfect and Hackathon Demos

    Teams present their ideas and projects to the judges.

  • 03:30 PM

    Meme Contest

    Results are shown live on the big screen.

  • 05:00 PM

    Prize Distribution and Closing

    Winners announced and certificates handed out.
