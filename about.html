About HackFest

A student-run tech fest that celebrates building, learning and teamwork.

Our Story

HackFest began as a small coding meet-up in the CSE department and has grown into a two-day fest where students from many colleges come together to build, compete and learn.

This year's theme is Tech for Campus Life: using technology to solve everyday problems that students face.

What to Expect

  • ✅ A 24-hour hackathon with mentors
  • ✅ Coding contests and a debug hunt
  • ✅ Hands-on workshops on Git and AI
  • ✅ Fun non-technical events
  • ✅ Certificates for every participant

Our Goals

Why we organise HackFest every year

🎓

Learn by Doing

Students learn faster by building real projects than by reading alone.

💡

Encourage Ideas

Every idea is welcome, from simple apps to bold startup plans.

🌱

Grow a Community

Connect freshers with seniors, mentors and fellow builders.
